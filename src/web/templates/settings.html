How Sign-In Works

Weebarr stays single-admin. Plex is the quick login path, and local auth remains the fallback you can keep enabled in parallel.

If both local auth and Plex are configured, the login screen always offers both options. If only one is configured, only that method is shown.

Saving a local account here never removes your claimed Plex user. It only adds or updates the username/password path.
